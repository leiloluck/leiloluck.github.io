**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** English

---

## Effects

Stimulant: increased energy, elevated mood, confidence, sociability. Low doses: energy/euphoria dominate. High doses: "tripping effects become more clear."

## Dosage

**Snorted:** Light 15–25 mg, Average 25–75 mg, Strong 75+ mg
**Swallowed:** Light 25–75 mg, Average 75–150 mg, Strong 150+ mg

## Duration

- **Snorted:** Onset 5–10 min; effects 2.5–4.5 hours
- **Swallowed:** Onset 20–60 min; longer duration

## Risks

**Short-term:** Compulsive redosing (stronger craving than speed/MDMA), overheating, dehydration, panic attacks, cardiovascular problems (heart attack, stroke), excited delirium, severe hangover lasting days.

**Long-term:** Addiction, depression, psychosis (especially with predisposition). As NPS, long-term data limited.

## Safer Use

1. Test drugs beforehand
2. Plan dosage/frequency in advance; commit to limits
3. Avoid when feeling unwell
4. Eat healthily before/after (antioxidants, vitamins)
5. Prevent overheating: breaks, ~1 glass water/hour
6. Avoid combining with alcohol/other substances
7. Contraindicated: diabetes, heart conditions, high BP, pregnancy, epilepsy, mental health disorders, MAOIs
8. Practice safe sex (empathogenic effects → forgotten protection)
