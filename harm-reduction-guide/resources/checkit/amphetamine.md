## Description

Stimulant belonging to the phenylethylamine class. Typically powder, consumed nasally or orally. Amphetamine content varies enormously; frequently mixed with caffeine, lactose, creatine.

## Effects

Increased wakefulness/alertness/energy, enhanced performance feeling, suppression of hunger/thirst/fatigue, reduced pain perception, increased urge to move and talk. Fight-or-flight activation.

## Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Oral | ~30 minutes | 6–12 hours |
| Nasal | A few minutes | 6–12 hours |

## Dosage

Average dose: **10–25 mg** (pure amphetamine). Street purity highly variable.

## Mechanism

Increased release of **dopamine** and **noradrenaline**, triggering adrenaline release. Blood pressure, heart rate, body temperature all rise.

## Risks

### Acute
- Body temperature rise, dehydration, jaw clenching, headaches
- At high doses: hallucinations and circulatory collapse
- Anxiety, aggression, overestimation of abilities

### Long-Term
- High psychological dependence potential
- High blood pressure, stomach/nasal mucosa damage
- Weight loss, nutritional deficiencies
- Depression, chronic restlessness
- **Amphetamine psychosis**: paranoia, hallucinations, delusions

## Contraindications
- Psychological problems, anxiety, high blood pressure
- Pregnancy/breastfeeding, cardiovascular problems
- Liver/kidney damage, epilepsy, hyperthyroidism

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Alcohol** | Masks alcohol effects → dangerous overestimation |
| **+ Cannabis** | Can "restart" speed effects; hallucinations, anxiety |
| **+ MDMA** | Amplified neurotoxicity; high fluid loss |
| **+ Other stimulants** | Extreme cardiovascular strain; life-threatening |

## Safer Use

1. Use drug checking services
2. Start very small, wait before redosing
3. Never share snorting tubes (hepatitis risk)
4. Oral consumption is least harmful route
5. Hydrate with ~0.5L/hour non-alcoholic drinks
6. Take rest breaks, seek fresh air
7. Stop consuming in time to allow recovery
8. Leave extended breaks between sessions
