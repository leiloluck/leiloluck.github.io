# Cocaine — Energy Control
**Source:** https://energycontrol.org/sustancias/cocaina/
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** Spanish (translated to English)

---

## Description

Derived from coca plant leaves. Three forms: cocaine hydrochloride (powder, snorted/injected), freebase/crack (smoked), basuco (base paste). Acts on dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin.

## Effects

Euphoria, suppression of fatigue/hunger, reduced inhibitions, increased HR/BP.

## Dosage (Intranasal)

| Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|
| 20–50 mg | 50–75 mg | >75 mg |

Start with ~25 mg test dose.

## Duration

Snorted: onset minutes, effects 30–45 min (stimulation up to 60–90 min).

## Risks

**Common:** Fatigue, irritability, insomnia, anxiety, appetite loss, comedown apathy. **Serious:** Anxiety/paranoia/hallucinations at high doses, cerebrovascular events, arrhythmias, dependence. **Overdose:** Severe anxiety, elevated temperature, tachycardia, potential cerebral/cardiac infarction.

## Drug Interactions

- **+ Stimulants**: Amplified effects, increased infarction risk
- **+ Alcohol**: Creates cocaethylene (cardiac/hepatic damage); masks intoxication
- **+ Psychedelics**: Cocaine may cancel psychedelic effects
- **+ Beta-blockers**: Greater adverse effects
- **+ Bupropion**: Avoid

## Safer Use

1. Start with 25 mg test dose; set your own limit in advance
2. Protect your nose: fine powder, clean surface, own tube, never banknotes
3. Clean nasal passages before/after with saline
4. Avoid frequent use; watch for dependence warning signs
5. Analyze substance beforehand — common adulterants: phenacetin, caffeine, lidocaine, levamisole
6. Especially risky with: mental health conditions, cardiovascular disease, pregnancy, epilepsy
7. If anxiety: calm ventilated space, deep breathing; resolves as effects wear off
