## Description

Dissociative substance producing consciousness-body separation. Hydrochloride salt, colorless/white crystalline powder. Two isomers: S-Ketamine (more potent) and R-Ketamine. Street samples may contain either racemic or single isomer — users cannot know which.

**Adulteration:** DMXE, 2-FDCK, cocaine, methamphetamine found in samples sold as ketamine.

## Routes & Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Intranasal | 5–15 min | 45–60 min |
| Oral | 5–20 min | ~90 min |
| IM/IV | Seconds to minutes | Variable |

## Dosage (Racemic Ketamine HCl)

**S-Ketamine: use HALF these amounts.**

| Route | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intranasal | 15–25 mg | 30–100 mg | 75–170 mg (K-hole) |
| Oral | 30–100 mg | 75–350 mg | 150–450 mg |
| IM | — | 50–130 mg | — |
| IV | — | 30–80 mg | — |

## Mechanism

NMDA receptor blockade → dissociation. Partial opioid activation → pain relief. Dopamine activation → euphoria. Antidepressant properties via neuronal dendrite growth.

## Effects

**Low dose:** Mild euphoria, alcohol-like, increased movement urge, floating sensation.
**Medium-high:** Progressive dissociation, emotional flattening, out-of-body experiences, hallucinations.
**K-hole:** Near-complete detachment; immobilized/helpless.

## Risks

**Acute:** BP elevation, cardiac arrhythmias, nausea, seizures, rhabdomyolysis, respiratory depression (especially with depressants).

**Long-term:** Cognitive impairment, urological damage (bladder, kidneys), liver damage, possible dependence.

## Dangerous Combinations
- **+ Depressants (alcohol, benzos, opioids, GHB):** ⚠️ Life-threatening respiratory depression
- **+ Stimulants (MDMA, cocaine, amphetamine):** Severe cardiovascular strain

## Safer Use

1. Use drug checking — cannot know if S or racemic
2. Plan session; medium/high doses in protected low-stimulus environment
3. Consume seated/lying to prevent fall injuries (pain is blocked)
4. Never use alone at higher doses; sober trip-sitter
5. Empty stomach (2 hours before) — aspiration risk
6. Own clean snorting tube
7. See urologist immediately if painful urination or blood in urine
