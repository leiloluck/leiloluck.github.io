## Description

MDMA (3,4-Methylenedioxymethamphetamine), phenethylamine family. First synthesized 1912 by Merck. Used in psychotherapy in the 1970s. Available as pressed tablets and crystal/powder.

## Desired Effects

Emotional amplification, empathy/closeness, enhanced self-esteem, euphoria, disinhibition, increased communication desire, enhanced music appreciation, desire for physical affection.

## Dosage

General guideline: **1–1.5 mg/kg body weight**.

| Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|
| 40–75 mg | 75–125 mg | >125 mg |

- Crystal: Pre-weigh doses in "bombs" or capsules. Never dip fingers.
- Pills: Modern pills often exceed 200 mg. **Always split pills.**
- Redosing: Max 250 mg total per session. 3–4 hours between doses.

## Duration

Onset: 20–70 minutes. Come-up: 5–20 minutes. Plateau: 2–3 hours. Total: 4–6 hours.

## Risks

**Onset:** Anxiety, nausea, dizziness. **During:** Dry mouth, mydriasis, jaw clenching, elevated HR/BP, thermoregulation issues. **After:** "MDMA hangover" 48–72 hours post-use — sadness, irritability, insomnia. **Chronic:** "Loss of the magic" with repeated use.

**Serious:** Hyperthermia (heat stroke — potentially fatal). Hyponatremia (more common in females).

## Drug Interactions

- **+ Alcohol**: Increased dehydration
- **+ Stimulants**: Amplified CNS stimulation
- **+ MAOIs (Moclobemide)**: ⚠️ Can be fatal
- **+ HIV meds (ritonavir)**: ⚠️ Dangerous MDMA blood level increases; deaths documented

## Safer Use

1. Heart conditions/hypertension: extreme caution or abstain
2. Moderate doses, space 4–6 weeks ("less is more")
3. Be patient — wait 20 min to 1+ hour for effects
4. Oral is least risky route
5. Not everything sold as ecstasy contains MDMA — use drug checking
6. Hydration: max 500 mL/hour; electrolyte drinks preferred
7. Take dancing breaks to cool down
8. If anxiety: calm ventilated space; reassure effects are temporary
9. Hyperthermia: medical emergency → immediate professional help
