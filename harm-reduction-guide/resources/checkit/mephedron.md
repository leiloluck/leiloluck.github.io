## Description

4-Methylmethcathinone (4-MMC, Mephedron) is chemically related to cathinone (khat plant) and methcathinone. Stimulating, euphoric, and empathogenic properties. Powder or tablet form. Classified as a "Research Chemical" — largely under-researched. Knowledge comes almost exclusively from user reports.

## Effects

Stimulation, euphoria, subjective mental clarity, heightened connectedness, intensified perception. Suppression of thirst, hunger, sleep.

## Mechanism

Increased release of serotonin, dopamine, and noradrenaline.

## Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Nasal (intense burning) | Minutes | ~1–2 hours |
| Oral | ~30–45 min | ~2–5 hours |

## Risks

### Acute
- Blood pressure elevation, rapid heartbeat
- High doses: delusions and paranoia
- Temperature changes, headaches, skin rashes
- Short-term memory impairment
- **Intense craving** — strong urge to redose (especially nasal)

### Long-Term
- Not yet sufficiently researched
- High psychological dependence potential
- Tolerance development
- Impaired memory and concentration

## Contraindications
- Psychological problems, anxiety, high blood pressure
- Pregnancy, cardiovascular problems
- Liver/kidney damage, epilepsy, HIV

## Safer Use

1. Only use when physically/psychologically healthy, in comfortable environment
2. Use own snorting tube; never use banknotes
3. Use drug checking services
4. Start low, wait before redosing
5. Avoid polydrug use, especially with other stimulants
6. Take breaks between uses (dependence risk)
