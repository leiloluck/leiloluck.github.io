## PsychonautWiki — DMT — https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/DMT — accessed 2026-07-24

N,N-DMT is a classic serotonergic psychedelic of the tryptamine class. **Vaporized/smoked dosing (freebase):** threshold 2 mg; light 10–20 mg; common 20–40 mg; strong 40–60 mg; heavy 60 mg+. **Smoked duration:** onset 20–40 seconds; come-up 1–3 minutes; peak 2–8 minutes; offset 1–6 minutes; after-effects 10–60 minutes; total 5–20 minutes. Orally administered DMT is **inactive** because gut monoamine oxidase (MAO) breaks it down; to be active orally it is combined with an MAO inhibitor (the ayahuasca/pharmahuasca context). **Toxicity/addiction:** "extremely low toxicity relative to dose," median lethal dose "has never been reached in any setting," relatively few acute physical side effects, though it "is thought to be able to act as a potential trigger for those with underlying psychiatric conditions." It is "considered to be non-addictive with a low abuse potential," with no animal self-administration reports, and "tolerance to the effects of DMT does not appear to occur." **Dangerous interactions:** Lithium — "significantly increases the risk of psychosis and seizures … strictly discouraged." Tramadol — "well-documented to lower the seizure threshold," and psychedelics may trigger seizures in susceptible individuals. Cannabis — "unexpectedly strong and unpredictable synergy" that can increase anxiety, paranoia, panic and psychosis. Stimulants — increased anxiety, paranoia, thought loops, mania and psychosis risk. **Trip-sitting:** "A trip sitter is recommended with this substance that often is smoked in heavy doses."

## DanceSafe — DMT — https://dancesafe.org/dmt/ — accessed 2026-07-24

DanceSafe describes DMT as "one of the most powerful psychedelics on the planet." **Smoked/vaped:** 10–40 mg, effects felt within seconds and peaking at 5–10 minutes; users take "two or three full hits to 'blast off,'" and holding the vapour in the lungs is "the most important (and difficult) part." **Oral (ayahuasca):** a brew combining a DMT-containing plant with an MAOI, because "DMT is destroyed in the stomach if consumed orally without an MAOI"; effects begin in 20–60 minutes and last 2–6 hours with lingering effects for several more hours. **Injection is discouraged** — "improper drug sourcing or injection methods can be catastrophic." **Psychological risk:** the rapid, overwhelming onset can be destabilising; users with a personal or family history of mood or psychotic disorders face "increased risk of psychological upset"; experiences may include complete ego dissolution and detachment from reality. **Harm reduction:** set and setting strongly influence outcomes — consuming with trusted people in a supportive environment reduces the chance of a difficult experience.

## TripSit — DMT (factsheet + combinations) — https://wiki.tripsit.me/wiki/DMT — accessed 2026-07-24

TripSit's DMT factsheet lists **smoked/vaporized** tiers of light 10–15 mg, common 15–25 mg, strong 25–40 mg+, with onset 0–3 minutes, peak 10–15 minutes, total 15–30 minutes (these run lower than PsychonautWiki's tiers — a real-world variance worth flagging). Taken orally, DMT "should be combined with a MAO-inhibitor such as Harmine," with dietary caution required when using MAOIs (avoid tyramine foods; reversible MAOIs need less restriction). **Combination matrix (TripSit `dmt` key):** Lithium — **Dangerous** ("a large number of reports indicating a high seizure and psychosis risk"). Tramadol — **Unsafe** ("lowers seizure threshold and psychedelics also cause occasional seizures"). Cocaine/amphetamines/mephedrone (stimulants) — **Caution** ("increase anxiety levels and the risk of thought loops"). Cannabis — **Caution** ("unexpectedly strong and somewhat unpredictable synergy"). MAOIs — listed **Low Risk & Synergy** (because for DMT the MAOI is the enabling co-drug), and SSRIs **Low Risk & Decrease** (SSRIs blunt smoked DMT). The lethal serotonergic/hypertensive danger arises from the MAOI combining with *other* serotonergics/stimulants/tyramine, not from DMT itself.

## Strassman RJ & Qualls CR 1994 — Dose-response study of N,N-DMT in humans, I: neuroendocrine, autonomic, cardiovascular effects (Arch Gen Psychiatry) — https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8297216/ — accessed 2026-07-24

The foundational human dose-response study. DMT was given intravenously at 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 and 0.4 mg/kg to 11 experienced hallucinogen users in a double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomised design. **Peak blood levels and subjective effects occurred within 2 minutes and were negligible by 30 minutes** — confirming the sharp, brief pharmacology also seen when smoked. DMT produced **dose-dependent increases in blood pressure, heart rate, pupil diameter and rectal (core) temperature**, and elevated beta-endorphin, corticotropin, cortisol and prolactin; growth hormone rose equally at all doses and melatonin was unaffected. Hallucinogenic threshold was 0.2 mg/kg and higher. Harm-reduction takeaway: the cardiovascular effect is genuine but **transient**, peaking within ~2 minutes and resolving quickly — relevant chiefly for people with pre-existing cardiac disease or uncontrolled hypertension. (Secondary clinical summaries cite systolic BP rises up to ~40 mmHg and heart rate up to ~119 bpm after bolus dosing; exact magnitudes for recreational vaporized use are not precisely established.)

## MAPS — MAOI Contraindications (relating to Ayahuasca) — https://maps.org/news-letters/v06n1/06158mao.html — accessed 2026-07-24

A canonical harm-reduction reference for the ayahuasca/MAOI risk profile — directly relevant because oral DMT requires an MAOI. Two principal dangers: **hypertensive crisis** ("tyramine stimulates the release of norepinephrine … a norepinephrine surge resulting in hypertension") and **serotonin syndrome**. Contraindicated drugs include amphetamines, cocaine, MDMA, meperidine (pethidine), and "sympathomimetic amines (direct & indirect acting) including pseudoephedrine & ephedrine." Fluoxetine (Prozac) is singled out as especially dangerous — MAOIs within five weeks of stopping it "could lead to coma and death"; other SSRIs need a two-to-three-week washout before MAOI intake. Tyramine-containing foods to avoid include aged cheese, liver, broad beans, dry/smoked/cured meat and fish, beer & ale, chocolate, sauerkraut, yeast extracts and caffeine. This is the source basis for the entry's core message: **smoked DMT alone is low-risk, but any MAOI-based preparation (or MAOI medication) plus another serotonergic/stimulant/tyramine load is potentially fatal.**

## Nayak SM et al. 2021 — Classic psychedelic coadministration with lithium (but not lamotrigine) is associated with seizures (Pharmacopsychiatry) — https://doi.org/10.1055/a-1524-2794 — accessed 2026-07-24

Analysis of online psychedelic experience reports (with Erowid) finding that co-administration of classic serotonergic psychedelics with **lithium** is associated with a markedly elevated rate of **seizures** (and psychotic reactions), whereas lamotrigine was not. This is the primary-literature basis for rating the DMT + lithium combination **Dangerous** and for the entry's ⚠️ hard-stop on that pairing. It underpins the TripSit "Dangerous" classification and PsychonautWiki's "strictly discouraged" language for psychedelics + lithium.
