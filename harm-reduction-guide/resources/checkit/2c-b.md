## Description

2C-B (4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine) is a synthetic psychedelic from the β-phenethylamine family. White powder, tablets, or capsules. Street names: Nexus, Bees, Venus, Bromo Mescaline. Described as combining MDMA and LSD qualities. Even a few milligrams can make a major difference.

## Effects

**Desired:** Mild euphoria, enhanced tactile sensitivity, warm body sensation, mildly stimulating, altered visual perception (kaleidoscopic patterns, color shifts), aphrodisiac properties.

**Negative:** Extreme temperature sensations, nausea, elevated blood pressure and temperature, accelerated heart rate. High doses: anxiety, disorientation, confusion. Nasal use causes intense burning.

## Dosage

**Extremely dose-dependent** — a few milligrams produce dramatically different experiences.

| Route | Light | Common | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oral | 5–15 mg | 15–30 mg | 25–50 mg |
| Nasal | ~Half of oral dose | | |

## Mechanism

Acts on serotonin receptors. Also likely increases dopamine and noradrenaline release.

## Risks

- Long-term consequences assumed comparable to other hallucinogens
- Dependence risk considered low
- May trigger anxiety disorders, schizophrenia, or depression in predisposed individuals
- **WARNING:** 2C-B-Fly and 2C-B-Dragonfly are more potent and barely researched

## Contraindications
- Anxiety, feeling unwell
- Psychiatric conditions
- Pregnancy, high blood pressure, epilepsy

## Safer Use

1. Use in comfortable, calm environment with trusted friends
2. Oral route is lowest-risk
3. Start low, go slow — steep dose-response curve
4. Use milligram-precision scale
5. Nasal route: much less needed but causes significant pain
6. Sexual safety: use condoms (aphrodisiac effects lower inhibitions)
7. Use drug checking services
