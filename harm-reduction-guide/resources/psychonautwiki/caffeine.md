# Caffeine — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Caffeine

## Description
Caffeine (also known as 1,3,7-Trimethylxanthine) is a naturally-occurring stimulant substance of the xanthine class. It is the most widely consumed psychoactive substance in the world. Found in seeds, leaves, and fruit of some plants. Most commonly consumed through coffee, tea, soft drinks, and energy drinks. 90% of adults in North America consume it daily.

## Subjective Effects
- Stimulation
- Wakefulness
- Enhanced focus
- Motivation enhancement

## Dosage (Oral)
- Threshold: 10 mg
- Light: 20–100 mg
- Common: 100–300 mg
- Strong: 300–500 mg
- Heavy: 500+ mg

## Duration (Oral)
- Total: 3–5 hours
- Onset: 5–10 minutes
- Come up: 15–30 minutes
- Peak: 1–2 hours
- Offset: 1–2 hours
- After effects: 2–6 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Not known to cause brain damage; extremely low toxicity relative to dose.
- No negative cognitive, psychiatric or toxic physical consequences in reasonable doses.
- In reasonable doses in careful context, presents no negative consequences.

## Safer Use
- Moderate intake to avoid anxiety and sleep disruption.
- Be mindful of caffeine content in energy drinks and supplements (may be much higher than coffee).
- Avoid late-day consumption to preserve sleep quality.
- Use harm reduction practices.
