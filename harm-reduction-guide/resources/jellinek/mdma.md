# XTC / MDMA — Jellinek
**Source:** https://www.jellinek.nl/en/alcohol-drugs-behavior/xtc-mdma/
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** English

---

## Effects

Stimulating and perception-altering. Entactogenic (social connection), euphoric ("in love"), energizing, perceptual changes (vivid colors, different music experience).

## Duration

Onset: 20–60 minutes. Total: 4–6 hours. Redosing doesn't extend positive effects and increases risks.

## Dosage (Weight-Based)

| Level | mg/kg | Example (70 kg) |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 0.5 | ~35 mg |
| Average | 1–1.5 | 70–105 mg |
| Strong | >1.5 | 105+ mg |

Pill appearance is NOT a reliable indicator of content/strength.

## Risks

**Short-term:** Overheating (hyperthermia), water poisoning (up to 24h post-use), cardiovascular problems (heart attack, stroke), seizures, excited delirium syndrome, "Tuesday dip" hangover.

**Long-term:** Potential brain harm, prolonged depression, memory damage.

## Safer Use

1. Get drugs tested beforehand
2. Only use when feeling good mentally/physically
3. Check medication interactions (some combinations life-threatening)
4. Hydration: ~1 glass/hour water/sports drink — NOT more (overhydration risk)
5. Prevent overheating: rest breaks, cool down
6. Do not combine with alcohol or other substances
7. Do not drive
8. Never use during pregnancy/breastfeeding
