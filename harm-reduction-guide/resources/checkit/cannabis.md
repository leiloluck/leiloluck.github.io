## Description

Cannabis encompasses hashish and marijuana, from the hemp plant. Primary psychoactive component: **THC** (tetrahydrocannabinol). Other cannabinoids include CBD and CBN. THC concentration highest in resin of female flower clusters.

## Effects

Altered time perception, intensified emotional states and sensory experiences. Enhanced well-being, mild euphoria, relaxation. At high doses (especially oral): hallucinations.

## Duration

- **Smoked:** ~1–4 hours
- **Oral:** ~4–10 hours
- After 20 hours, half of THC remains in bloodstream

## Mechanism

THC binds to cannabinoid receptors in the brain governing memory, time perception, hunger, coordination, movement. CBD can reduce unpleasant psychological effects.

## Risks

### Acute
- Increased heart rate, red eyes, dry mouth, dizziness, nausea
- Reduced cognitive performance (memory, attention, concentration)
- Higher doses: circulatory problems, mild hallucinations, paranoia

### Long-Term
- Respiratory issues from chronic smoking (bronchitis, lung inflammation)
- Reduced motivation with daily long-term use
- Short-term memory impaired 6–12 weeks after stopping daily use
- Psychological dependence possible with very frequent use

## Contraindications
- Lung disease, heart conditions
- Anxiety, depression, psychological problems
- Pregnancy/breastfeeding

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Stimulants** | Greater body stress; prolonged comedown |
| **+ Psychedelics (LSD, mushrooms)** | Significantly increased confusion, anxiety, paranoia |

## Safer Use

1. Use activated carbon filters or vaporizer to reduce lung damage
2. Oral consumption: onset delayed up to 1 hour; stronger and longer effects — wait before redosing
3. Only use in positive mental state
4. Do not use at work/school/while studying
5. Do not drive after consuming
