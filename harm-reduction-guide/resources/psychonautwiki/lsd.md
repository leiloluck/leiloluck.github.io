# LSD — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/LSD

## Description
Lysergic acid diethylamide (also known as Lysergide, LSD-25, LSD, L, Lucy, and Acid) is a classical psychedelic substance of the lysergamide class. It is perhaps the most researched and culturally influential psychedelic substance, as well as the prototypal lysergamide. The mechanism of action is not fully known, although serotonin binding activity is thought to be involved.

## Subjective Effects
- Visual geometry / hallucinatory states
- Time distortion
- Enhanced introspection
- Conceptual thinking
- Increased music appreciation
- Euphoria
- Ego loss

## Dosage (Sublingual)
- Threshold: 15 µg
- Light: 25–75 µg
- Common: 75–150 µg
- Strong: 150–300 µg
- Heavy: 300+ µg

## Duration (Sublingual)
- Total: 8–12 hours
- Onset: 15–30 minutes
- Come up: 45–90 minutes
- Peak: 3–5 hours
- Offset: 3–5 hours
- After effects: 12–48 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Physiologically well-tolerated with extremely low toxicity relative to dose.
- No evidence for long-lasting effects on the brain or other organs.
- No documented deaths attributed to direct LSD toxicity.
- Can strongly impair judgment and attention span, promoting erratic or dangerous behaviors.
- Extreme negative reactions ("bad trips") can cause lasting psychological trauma if not properly managed.
- Evidence for mental health treatment effectiveness only applies to controlled clinical settings; self-medicating may worsen issues.

## Safer Use
- Use under supervision of a tripsitter for first time or higher doses.
- Start with a low-common dose and monitor for unusual reactions (mania, delusions).
- Do not exceed the heavy dose range; take tolerance breaks instead.
- Use a reagent testing kit to verify genuine LSD (not dangerous counterfeits).
- Keep benzodiazepines or antipsychotics available to abort the trip if needed.
- Use harm reduction practices.
