## Description

LSD-25 (lysergic acid diethylamide), synthesized 1938 by Albert Hofmann at Sandoz. Colorless, odorless, slightly bitter. Taken orally on blotter paper, sugar cubes, or microdots. Highly sensitive — degrades with light, heat, air, moisture.

## Effects

**Mood:** Emotional susceptibility — intense swings. Euphoria may evolve to omnipotence feelings. Anxiety may increase or decrease.

**Sensory:** Greater sensitivity to sound/visuals, body image distortions, visual distortions/illusions, synesthesia, altered time perception.

**Cognitive:** Stimulated thought processes, disrupted/creative thinking, feelings of unity, mystical experiences.

Effects are highly unpredictable — depend on context and mental state.

## Dosage (µg)

| Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|
| 20–30 µg | 100 µg | 150–200 µg |

Illegal market provides no dosage info — consume only a fraction of a blotter initially.

## Duration

Total: 6–11 hours. Onset: 20–30 min. Peak/trip: 3–6 hours. Comedown: gradual through 8–12 hours.

## Risks

- Rapid tolerance with frequent use
- Elevated HR/BP
- **Psychological risks primary** — difficult trips with unpleasant hallucinations, agitation, paranoia (time-limited but can last 24+ hours)
- Controversy on permanent psychiatric effects. Traumatic trips could theoretically trigger schizophrenia in vulnerable individuals.

## Drug Interactions

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Alcohol** | Clouds experience; less controllable |
| **+ Stimulants** | Amplifies effects; increased adverse reaction risk |
| **+ Cannabis** | Increases psychedelic effects; potentially overwhelming |
| **+ Lithium, tricyclics, ritonavir** | ⚠️ Avoid — seizures reported |

## Safer Use

1. **Set and setting**: Not during difficult personal period; pleasant safe environment
2. Handle all obligations beforehand
3. Start with test dose or moderate amount; low doses for parties, high doses for calm settings only
4. Maintain caution through comedown (false security)
5. Use in company; inform companions
6. Minimum 2 weeks between uses (tolerance)
7. Handling difficult trips: walk, redirect thoughts, seek help; if intense/persistent → structured support
8. Analyze substance beforehand — other psychedelics sometimes sold as LSD
9. Especially risky with: emotional difficulties, mental health conditions, pregnancy, developmental age
