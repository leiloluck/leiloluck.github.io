## Selected Alerts

### 4-CMC (Clephedron), sold as 3-MMC (23 March 2026)
- **Found:** 4-CMC-HCl at 92.8%; NO 3-MMC detected
- **Appearance:** White powder
- **Risk:** Poorly researched cathinone. Neurotoxicity in cell cultures. One death reported. Side effects: hyperthermia, elevated HR/BP, jaw clenching.

### N-Ethylpentedron, sold as 3-MMC (multiple samples)
- **Found:** N-Ethylpentedron-HCl; NO 3-MMC detected
- **Risk:** Virtually no scientific data. Strong compulsive redosing. "Consumption strongly discouraged."

### 3-CMC, sold as 4-MMC (23 March 2026)
- **Found:** 3-CMC-HCl at 94.6%; NO 4-MMC detected
- **Risk:** Very strong craving potential. Likely neurotoxic. Paranoia at high doses.

### Tusi/Tucibi ("Pink Cocaine") (10 March 2026)
- **Found:** MDMA-HCl 19.3%, Ketamine-HCl 36.9%, Caffeine 3.5%, insoluble particles
- **Appearance:** Pink powder
- **Risk:** MDMA-ketamine combination heavily stresses cardiovascular system. Insoluble particles pose additional danger for all routes.

### "Philipp Plein" High-dose MDMA Tablet (16 March 2026)
- **Found:** 125.9 mg MDMA-HCl
- **Appearance:** Blue tablet, 468.6 mg
- **Risk:** Overdose: hyperthermia, dehydration, circulatory failure. Safe thresholds: women 1.3 mg/kg, men 1.5 mg/kg.

### Speed Contaminated with Phenethylamine (17 March 2026)
- **Found:** Amphetamine 0.9%, Caffeine 40.6%, Phenethylamine 6.0%
- **Risk:** Almost no actual amphetamine. Dangerous with MAO inhibitors.

### 2C-B Tablet Contaminated with 2C-H (16 March 2026)
- **Found:** 2C-B-HCl 24.1 mg + 2C-H-HCl
- **Risk:** 2C-H is a synthesis precursor that may inhibit MAO-B, potentially amplifying 2C-B effects unpredictably.
