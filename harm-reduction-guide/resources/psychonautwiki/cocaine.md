# Cocaine — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Cocaine

## Description
Cocaine (also known as benzoylmethylecgonine, coke, cola, snow, blow, white) is a classical stimulant substance of the tropane class. It is a naturally-occurring alkaloid extracted from the leaves of two coca plant species native to South America. The mechanism of action involves increasing levels of serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine in the brain.

## Subjective Effects
- Stimulation
- Euphoria
- Enhanced focus
- Increased confidence
- Appetite suppression
- Compulsive redosing

## Dosage (Insufflated)
- Threshold: 5 mg
- Light: 10–30 mg
- Common: 30–60 mg
- Strong: 60–90 mg
- Heavy: 90+ mg

## Duration (Insufflated)
- Total: 10–90 minutes
- Onset: 1–10 minutes
- Come up: 5–15 minutes
- Peak: 15–30 minutes
- Offset: 10–30 minutes
- After effects: 1–4 hours

## Adulterants
- By 2017, 87% of cocaine seized in the US contained levamisole.
- Levamisole is linked to cocaine/levamisole-associated vasculitis (CLIV), levamisole-induced necrosis syndrome (LINES), and cocaine/levamisole-associated autoimmune syndrome (CLAAS).
- DanceSafe recommends testing cocaine with Morris > Marquis > Liebermann reagent sequence.

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Chronic use leads to neurotoxicity and is associated with high morbidity and mortality rates.
- Most serious physical effects are cardiovascular: severe cardiac events, sudden cardiac death at high doses due to cardiac sodium channel blocking.
- Long-term use may result in cocaine-related cardiomyopathy.
- Regular insufflation can cause loss of smell, nosebleeds, difficulty swallowing, hoarseness, chronically runny nose.
- "Crack lung" can develop even after short-term or initial use of smoked cocaine.
- Sharing snorting equipment increases risk of transmitting blood-borne diseases (hepatitis C, HIV).

## Safer Use
- Prepare substances into fine powder before use.
- Always use your own clean snorting tool — never share.
- Limit use per nostril per session.
- Rinse nose with saline within 30–60 minutes after use.
- Consider buccal administration as a harm reduction alternative.
- Use reagent testing kits to check for adulterants.
- Use harm reduction practices.
