# Page 1: Material List (MATERIAL LISTE ELEKTRO)

This page contains a handwritten material list in German for the electrical installation.

## Transcribed Items & Interpretation:
- **KIR ROHR M 20 (100 m) + BRIDEN**: 100 meters of KIR conduit pipe (size M20) along with mounting brackets (Briden).
- **Waco klemmen 3er/5er (200 stk / 100 stk)**: WAGO wire connectors. 200 pieces of 3-wire connectors and 100 pieces of 5-wire connectors.
- **DOSEN T13 2fach 1 mal geschaltet (17 Stk)**: 17 pieces of Type 13 (T13, standard Swiss socket) double wall sockets, where one socket is switched. (The number 18 was crossed out and corrected to 17).
- **Dosen T13 2fach (15 Stk) | Schrauben**: 15 pieces of regular Type 13 double sockets, along with a note for screws.
- **Schalter S3 2fach (2 Stk)**: 2 pieces of double switches (Scheme 3 / S3). 
  - *Side Notes*: "Waschmaschine" (Washing machine), "Nass? ein Sicherung?" (Wet area? a fuse?), "Boiler direkt anschluss" (Boiler direct connection), "Kabel Schalteranschlussdose" (Cable switch connection box).
- **Schalter S3/S0 (4 Stk)**: 4 pieces of switches (S3/S0).
  - *Side Note*: "Lage Bad Durchgang" (Location: Bath / Passage).
- **1 Dose Aussen S x T13 Nass**: 1 outdoor wet-area socket (S x T13).
  - *Side Note*: "Aussen steckdose" (Outside socket).
- **Abzweigdosen nicht zu klein (20 Stk)**: 20 junction boxes, with a specific note that they should "not be too small" (nicht zu klein).
  - *Side Note*: "LAMPEN: 3 Decke, 1 Aussen" (Lamps: 3 ceiling, 1 outside).

## Wiring Color Code Reference (Bottom Left):
- **T Draht** (T Wire)
- **Erde** (Earth/Ground): **grün/gelb** (green/yellow)
- **Null** (Neutral): **blau** (blue)
- **Phase**: **braun** (brown)
- **gesch. Phase** (Switched Phase): **weiss, orange** (white, orange)
- **schema 3**: **rosa / hellgrün** (pink / light green)
