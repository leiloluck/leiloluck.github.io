## Substance Description

Methamphetamine is "a potent stimulant" that typically circulates as hydrochloride salt in colorless crystalline form, highly water-soluble. It exists in R- and S-forms; S-methamphetamine is approximately four times more potent. Crystal exhibits "very high" purity levels, with median active content around 98.1% as methamphetamine hydrochloride.

## Routes of Administration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Sniffing (nasal) | 1-5 minutes | 6-30 hours |
| Oral (swallowing) | ~30 minutes | 6-30 hours |
| Intravenous | Few seconds | 6-30 hours |
| Inhalation/smoking | Few seconds | 6-30 hours |
| Anal/rectal | Delayed vs IV | 6-30 hours |

Duration varies by dosage and individual tolerance.

## Dosage Ranges

- **Low:** 2-10 mg (oral/nasal)
- **Moderate:** 10-25 mg (oral/nasal)

These are not consumption recommendations; individual factors significantly affect tolerance.

## Mechanisms & Effects

Methamphetamine releases dopamine, noradrenaline, and serotonin (at high doses) from neurons. Effects include "increased wakefulness, attention and concentration," suppressed hunger/fatigue/pain, elevated blood sugar, increased speech/movement drive, confidence, and potential euphoria.

## Acute Side Effects

- Elevated body temperature, blood pressure, heart rate; palpitations
- Vasoconstriction in extremities
- Dilated pupils, blurred vision, dry mouth
- Accelerated breathing
- Headache, dizziness, nausea, vomiting
- Jaw muscle hyperactivity, tremors, muscle cramps
- Urinary retention, erectile dysfunction
- Rhabdomyolysis
- Restlessness, aggressive behavior, paranoia, suicidal ideation, panic attacks
- Stroke, seizures, cardiovascular collapse, sudden death

## Aftereffects

Unrest, irritability, concentration problems, depression, exhaustion, insomnia, intense hunger.

## Long-term Consequences

Psychotic states, paranoia, hallucinations, depression, personality changes, weight loss, skin inflammation, nasal damage, hypertension, heart damage, kidney/liver damage, menstrual irregularities, brain nerve cell damage, tolerance, and dependence.

## Detection Windows

- **Blood:** 24+ hours
- **Saliva:** 2-3 days
- **Urine:** 2-7 days

## Contraindications

Do not consume with: psychotic conditions, anorexia, MAOI antidepressants, cardiovascular disease, arterial disease, post-stroke, liver/kidney damage, epilepsy, diabetes, asthma, hyperthyroidism, glaucoma, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or before driving.

## Safer Use Guidance

- Obtain drug checking analysis before consumption
- Start with small amounts ("test dose")
- Avoid repeated doses immediately
- Plan consumption duration in advance
- Maintain consumption breaks (weeks between weekend use)
- Drink electrolyte/vitamin beverages regularly
- Consume light foods/fruits
- Take cooling breaks at events
- Avoid polysubstance use, especially alcohol (overdose risk)
- Use personal, rounded straws (sniffing) to prevent infection
- Employ sterile equipment exclusively (injecting)
- Never drive under influence

## Drug Interactions

Substances inhibiting liver enzyme CYP2D6 (fluoxetine, sertraline, paroxetine, duloxetine, bupropion, others) dangerously potentiate and prolong methamphetamine effects.
