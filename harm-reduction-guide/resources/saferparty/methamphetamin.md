**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** German (translated to English)

## Description

Methamphetamine (N-methylamphetamine) is a synthetic amphetamine derivative. It reaches the brain faster than amphetamine and produces a stronger, longer-lasting high with greater addiction potential.

## Effects

Triggers simultaneous release of noradrenaline and dopamine, causing:
- "Pronounced subjective performance enhancement, increased self-esteem, euphoria, disinhibition and increased lust"
- Elevated body temperature, accelerated pulse/respiration, elevated blood pressure
- Suppressed hunger, sleep need, and pain perception
- Increased risk-taking, aggression, extreme nervousness, and excessive talkativeness
- Slow bodily breakdown of the substance

## Duration by Route

**Onset:**
- Snorted: 10-20 minutes
- Oral: 30-120 minutes
- Smoked/injected: seconds

**Duration:** 3-12 hours (high doses may exceed 24 hours)
**Aftereffects:** up to 70 hours

## Dosage Ranges

| Route | Light | Moderate | Strong | Very Strong |
|-------|-------|----------|--------|-------------|
| **Oral** | 5-10mg | 10-25mg | 25-50mg | 50mg+ |
| **IV** | 5-10mg | 10-30mg | 30-40mg | 40mg+ |
| **Nasal** | 5-10mg | 10-30mg | 30-50mg | 50mg+ |
| **Smoked** | 5-10mg | 10-25mg | 25-50mg | 50mg+ |

**Forms:** Pills (Yaba, Pervitin) or crystalline powder

## Short-Term Risks

- Jaw clenching, dry mouth, dehydration
- Tachycardia, hypertension, tremors
- Irritability, aggressive/violent behavior (especially with alcohol/high doses)
- Psychiatric symptoms: agitation, suicidal ideation, psychotic reactions
- Overdose fatalities from pulmonary edema, cerebral hemorrhage, arrhythmias, acute heart failure, or severe fever

## Long-Term Risks

"Very high addiction potential with psychological and physical withdrawal symptoms." Chronic use may cause:
- Pronounced comedowns, aggression, depression, intense cravings
- Persistent restlessness, sleep/circulation disorders, paranoia, amphetamine psychosis
- Frequent suicidal thoughts
- Severe weight loss, skin infections ("speed pimples"), tooth decay
- Kidney/lung damage, gastric issues, seizures
- Menstrual cycle disruption (women)
- Tissue damage from specific routes (nasal mucosa from snorting; gastric from oral; lung damage/cancer risk from smoking)
- Increased risk of stroke and cardiac damage
- Suspected irreversible brain changes affecting intellectual capacity
- Significantly elevated Parkinson's disease risk in chronic users

## Safer Use Guidelines

- "Use low doses and do not redose; methamphetamine is a highly potent substance"
- Eat adequately before/after use; supplement with vitamins C, D and minerals (iron, calcium, magnesium)
- Note: full stomach delays oral absorption
- Take breaks of at least four weeks between use due to severe comedowns
- Plan adequate time for consumption and recovery
- Avoid if experiencing psychiatric conditions, hypertension, liver/kidney disease, epilepsy, diabetes, or pregnancy

## Drug Combinations

**With Depressants** (alcohol, GHB, opioids, benzodiazepines, ketamine):
- Heart strain and circulatory collapse risk
- Masked effects increase overdose danger

**With Other Stimulants** (cocaine, amphetamine):
- Severe cardiovascular stress
- Unpredictable potentiation of stimulant effects
- Elevated risk of tachycardia and respiratory distress
