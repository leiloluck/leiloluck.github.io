# Energy Control — Resource Collection

**Organization:** Energy Control (program of Asociación Bienestar y Desarrollo / ABD)
**Website:** https://energycontrol.org
**Location:** Spain (Cataluña, Andalucía, Madrid, Islas Baleares)
**Language:** Spanish
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31

## About

Energy Control is one of Europe's leading harm reduction programs focused on recreational drug use. They provide:
- Drug checking / substance analysis services (in-person and by mail)
- International drug checking service (energycontrol-international.org)
- Comprehensive substance information
- Drug alerts
- Outreach at festivals and nightlife events

Operating since the late 1990s, they are a key reference for Southern European harm reduction.

## Content Structure

Each substance page on energycontrol.org contains:
- **Descripción** (Description) — history, chemistry, forms
- **Efectos deseados** (Desired effects)
- **Dosificación** (Dosage) — route-specific dose ranges
- **Duración** (Duration) — onset, peak, total
- **Riesgos** (Risks) — common, serious, overdose
- **Interacciones** (Drug interactions) — detailed combination warnings
- **Recomendaciones** (Harm reduction recommendations) — extensive, practical guidelines
