# Drugchecking Berlin — Resource Collection

**Organization:** Drugchecking Berlin

**Funded by:** Berlin Senate Department for Science, Health, and Care
**Evaluated by:** Charité Berlin
**Language:** German
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31

## About

Berlin's publicly funded drug checking service providing:
- Anonymous, free chemical analysis of psychoactive substances
- Neutral substance information, effects, side effects, and risk counseling
- Current drug warnings/alerts when substances pose exceptional risk
- Operated by three partner organizations: Fixpunkt gGmbH, Schwulenberatung Berlin, vista - Misfit

## Content Structure

Each substance page typically contains:
- **Substanz** (Description) — chemical class, forms, adulterants found
- **Konsumformen** (Routes of administration) — with onset and duration
- **Dosierungen** (Dosage) — route-specific dose ranges
- **Wirkungen** (Effects) — mechanism, desired effects
- **Nebenwirkungen** (Side effects) — acute, after-effects, long-term
- **Safer Use** — harm reduction guidelines
- **Wechselwirkungen** (Drug combinations) — interaction warnings
- **Nachweiszeiten** (Detection times) — blood, urine, saliva
- **Kontraindikationen** (Contraindications)
- **Strategien für Sex unter Substanzeinfluss** (Sexual health/chemsex)

## Drug Alerts

The `/aktuelle-warnungen` page publishes warnings for substances with exceptional risk. Key patterns (March 2026):
- Massive mislabeling in synthetic cathinone market (3-MMC/4-MMC sold but not present)
- N-Ethylpentedron and 2-MMC frequently substituted for mephedron
- High-dose MDMA tablets circulating
- Tusi/Tucibi ("pink cocaine") MDMA-ketamine combinations found
