## Description

Contains THC (primary psychoactive compound). Forms: marijuana (dried flowering tops), hashish (resin). Can be smoked, vaporized, or eaten (edibles).

## Effects

Cheerful, relaxed feeling. Intensified sensory perception (music, colors). Intensifies existing mood (can worsen bad mood). Elevated heart rate, bloodshot eyes. Impairs logical thinking and short-term memory.

## Duration

- **Smoked/vaporized:** Minutes to onset, 2–4 hours
- **Edibles:** ~1 hour onset, 4–8 hours

## Edible Warning

People "think it's not working and eat another piece" — then intensity surprises and frightens them.

## Risks

**Short-term:** Dizziness, nausea, fainting, panic attacks, cannabis psychosis/bad trip. Fatal overdose essentially impossible (THC doesn't affect breathing/heartbeat centers).

**Long-term:** Delayed development, chronic fatigue, poor concentration, weakened immunity, bronchitis, possible lung cancer risk, greater psychosis risk (predisposed individuals), dependence (tolerance builds; withdrawal up to 2 weeks).

## Safer Use

1. Start low, go slow — especially edibles/high-THC
2. Wait at least 1 hour before redosing edibles
3. Avoid when already feeling bad
4. Younger users face higher risk
5. Monitor frequency — heavier/more frequent = more problems
