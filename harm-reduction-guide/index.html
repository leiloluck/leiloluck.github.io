🆘 Emergency & first aid

Call now — 112 (EU) · 999 UK · 911 US/CA · 000 AU

Call if someone is unconscious and can't be woken · breathing is slow, irregular or has stopped, or lips look blue · is having a seizure lasting over 5 minutes · is burning-up hot and confused (heatstroke) · is rigid, twitching or feverish after MDMA (serotonin syndrome).

While you wait: if they are unconscious but breathing, put them in the recovery position (on their side) and tilt the head back to keep the airway open. If they are not breathing, start CPR (30 chest compressions to 2 breaths; hands-only is fine). For a suspected opioid overdose, give naloxone if you have it — then still call. Stay with them and tell responders what was taken (they are bound by confidentiality).

Source: Drugchecking Berlin — First Aid / Erste Hilfe

Estimated Risk Profile 📊

These are qualitative editorial estimates (None → Very High), not measured figures, and they show a fixed profile — they are not scaled by the dose you pick above (higher doses raise every risk, especially overdose). The source-linked detail lives in Common Risks (above) and the Protocol & Combinations tabs.

How these ratings are decided

Scale: each bar is rated None / Low / Moderate / High / Very High — deliberately coarse, because the underlying judgement has no finer precision.

Axes: Overdose / Lethality — risk of death from the drug itself (overdose, breathing failure, narrow dose-to-danger margin); Neurotoxicity — lasting damage to neural tissue; Cardiotoxicity — heart-rate, blood-pressure and vascular strain; Dehydration — fluid and electrolyte disruption; Sleep Disruption — how strongly it prevents or degrades sleep around use; Compulsion — loss of behavioural control (disinhibition and compulsive redosing).

Ratings summarise the sourced Common Risks for each substance. The Overdose / Lethality axis follows the "drug-specific mortality" dimension from the peer-reviewed harm analysis of Nutt, King & Phillips (2010), which is also the source of the independent overall-harm ranking shown with each chart.
