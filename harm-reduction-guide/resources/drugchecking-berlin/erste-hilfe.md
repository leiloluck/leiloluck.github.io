## General Principles

First aid is always delivered based on **symptoms**, not substances. Don't be afraid of doing something wrong — doing nothing can be life-threatening.

## Emergency Number: 112

### The "5 Ws" for Emergency Calls
- WHERE did it happen?
- WHAT happened?
- HOW MANY injured?
- WHAT INJURIES?
- WAIT for follow-up questions

You only need to describe symptoms — not obligated to mention drugs. When medical staff arrive, inform them about substances consumed (they have confidentiality).

---

## Finding an Unresponsive Person

1. Stay calm
2. Secure the scene (remove hazards)
3. Check consciousness (speak loudly, shake, mild pain stimulus)
4. Check breathing
5. If unconscious/not breathing → call 112 immediately

---

## Circulatory Shock

**Causes:** Depressant overdoses, allergic reactions, severe stress, physical overexertion.
**Symptoms:** Pale complexion, cold sweats, restlessness, dizziness, nausea, brief unconsciousness.
**Response:** Lay flat, elevate legs. Monitor breathing. If no improvement → call 112.

## Unconsciousness (Breathing Present)

**Response:** Recovery position → call 112 → keep warm → monitor breathing continuously. If they regain consciousness, do NOT give food.

## Respiratory/Cardiac Arrest

**Response:** Call 112 IMMEDIATELY → CPR: 30 compressions + 2 breaths → continue until ambulance arrives. If alone/unsure: compressions only.

### Naloxone for Opioid Overdose
Nasal spray (Nyxoid) reverses opioid respiratory depression within seconds. Only works for opioids. Still call 112 even after administering.

## Bad Trips (Psychedelics: LSD, Mushrooms, 2C-B)

1. **Create safety:** Involve trusted people, quiet place, identify yourself, stay with them
2. **Provide orientation:** Tell time elapsed, location. Reassure the trip will end.
3. **Promote well-being:** Offer water (NEVER coffee/alcohol/energy drinks). Manage temperature. Calm breathing/paper bag for hyperventilation.
4. **Get help** if unable to manage → call emergency services

## Acute Psychosis (Stimulants: Speed, Crystal, Cocaine)

**Symptoms:** Irritability, hallucinations, paranoia, delusions (from sleep deprivation + high-dose stimulant use).
**Response:** Stay calm. Explain it's a brain fatigue reaction. Reassure it will pass with rest/sleep. Find calming activities. No additional substances. Only emergency physicians can administer appropriate sedatives.

---

## Seizures

**Response:** Clear area of objects. NEVER restrain. NEVER put objects between teeth. If seizure >5 minutes or first seizure → call 112. After: recovery position, check breathing.

## Psychomotor Seizure (High-dose Stimulants)

**Symptoms:** Aimless automatic movements (chewing, scratching, tapping, head wobbling).
**Response:** Protect from injury. Try to guide to sit and speak soothingly. If no improvement in 30 minutes → call 112.

---

## Serotonin Syndrome

**Causes:** Excessive MDMA dose, or MDMA + antidepressants/MAOIs/serotonergic substances.
**Symptoms:** Fever, chills, trembling, muscle twitching, diarrhea, high BP, loss of coordination, heart rhythm disturbances, confusion.
**Response:** Call 112 IMMEDIATELY. Inform responders what was consumed.

## Heat Stroke

**Causes:** Stimulant use + dancing + dehydration + sun.
**Symptoms:** Bright red hot face, hot skin, rapid heartbeat, nausea.
**Response:** Move to cool area, provide water + minerals. Cool with damp cloths (NOT too rapidly — cardiac arrest risk). If no improvement → call 112.
