## Description

Ethanol (ethyl alcohol) — clear, colorless, flammable liquid. Produced through fermentation (up to 18% ABV); distillation raises to ~80%. Distillation may produce toxic byproducts (methanol, fusel oils). Likely the oldest human-produced psychoactive substance.

## Effects

**Low doses:** Well-being, relaxation, sociability, reduced anxiety, lowered inhibitions.
**Higher doses:** Primarily depressant/sedative; sexual function impaired.

## Mechanism

20% absorbed through stomach; most via small intestine. Peak BAC: 30–60 minutes after consumption. Average metabolic rate: **~0.15‰ per hour**. Nothing accelerates breakdown.

## Blood Alcohol Levels

| Permille (‰) | Effects |
|---|---|
| 0.2–1.2 | Disinhibition, impulsivity, impaired coordination |
| 1.3–3.0 | Severe motor impairment, visual disturbances, nausea |
| 3.0+ | Hypothermia or hyperthermia, deep sleep |
| 4.0+ | Coma and death possible |

## Dosage

- <100g pure alcohol per week = largely low-risk
- 20g ≈ 0.5L beer, 0.25L wine, or 3 small shots
- Women achieve ~1/5 higher BAC than men at equivalent doses

## Risks

- Hangover, blackout (memory loss — always a sign of overconsumption)
- Dependence typically develops after years of regular use
- Cultural normalization causes risk minimization

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Cannabis** | Amplified effects; impaired motor skills; unconsciousness |
| **+ Tobacco** | Increased cancer risk long-term |
| **+ MDMA** | Liver/kidney strain; fluid loss; overheating |
| **+ Stimulants** | False sense of sobriety while heavily intoxicated |
| **+ Downers (GHB, benzos, opioids)** | ⚠️ Life-threatening respiratory depression |

## Safer Use

1. Alternate alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks
2. Next day: light foods, plenty of fluids
3. Do not drive
