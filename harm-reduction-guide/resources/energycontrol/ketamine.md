# Ketamine — Energy Control
**Source:** https://energycontrol.org/sustancias/ketamina/
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** Spanish (translated to English)

---

## Description

Dissociative anesthetic first synthesized 1962. NMDA receptor antagonist. Related to PCP. Among most widely used anesthetics globally.

## Effects

Relaxation, euphoria, reduced pain/fatigue, mental disconnect, out-of-body sensations, visual/auditory hallucinations, disinhibition, altered time perception.

## Dosage

| Route | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oral | 50–100 mg | 100–300 mg | — |
| Nasal | 15–30 mg | 30–75 mg | >75 mg |

For full psychedelic/K-hole: ~200 mg nasal, ~300 mg oral.

## Duration

Nasal onset: 7.5–20 min. Effects: 1–2 hours. Short-acting.

## Risks

**Frequent:** Motor impairment, nausea, dizziness, confusion, amnesia. **Serious:** Tolerance/psychological dependence, bladder/urinary system damage (early symptoms reversible; continued use → potential bladder destruction), biliary dilation, overwhelming psychedelic experiences (NDEs, catatonic states).

## Drug Interactions

- **+ Depressants (alcohol, GHB, opioids)**: ⚠️ Strongly discouraged — potentiated sedation, loss of coordination, fainting
- **+ Stimulants**: Substances mask each other → overconsumption of both
- **+ Psychedelics**: Extra caution required

## Safer Use

1. Watch dose carefully — start with small test dose; lines smaller than for other substances
2. Use in company; inform companions
3. Protect nasal passages: grind thoroughly, own clean tube, saline rinse
4. Avoid frequent use; take breaks when tolerance builds
5. Test substance beforehand
6. Stay hydrated (helps prevent bladder crystallization damage)
7. Especially risky with cardiac problems, hypertension, mental health conditions, pregnancy
8. Difficult trips: walk, redirect attention, seek help; if persistent → professional assistance
