## Description

LSD (lysergic acid diethylamide) is a potent psychedelic consumed orally. Forms: liquid drops, "microdots" (small pills), or blotter paper ("trips"). Semi-synthetic, first synthesized in the 1930s by Albert Hofmann from ergot fungus. Psychoactive properties discovered accidentally in April 1943. Dosage varies significantly per tab due to uneven liquid application.

## Effects

**Onset:** 30–90 minutes. **Peak:** ~2–4 hours. **Total duration:** Up to 12 hours.

**Physical:** Mild increase in body temperature, pulse, blood pressure, blood sugar. Pupil dilation, lightheadedness.

**Psychological:** Intensification/alteration of all senses, synesthesia, visual patterns, distorted spatial/time perception, amplification of moods, unconscious psychological states may resurface.

## Risks

- Perceptual overwhelm → confusion, disorientation
- **Bad trip / horror trip**: severe anxiety, panic, paranoia
- **Flashbacks**: Days/weeks later, brief recurrences of sensory distortions
- Can trigger latent psychiatric conditions (depression, schizophrenia)
- Physical tolerance builds (no physical dependence)

## Contraindications
- Psychiatric issues or mental illness history
- Cardiovascular problems, seizure disorders, epilepsy
- Pregnancy (vasoconstriction, uterine contractions)

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Psilocybin** | Cross-tolerance; stronger hallucinations; cardiovascular strain |
| **+ MDMA** | Very high serotonin levels; depression risk; dangerous overheating |
| **+ Cannabis** | Can restart LSD effects; frightening hallucinations |
| **+ Nicotine/Alcohol** | Circulatory burden; dizziness/fainting |

## Safer Use

1. Only consume when mentally/physically well, in safe environment
2. Start low, wait 1–2 hours before considering redose
3. Plan for 12 hours — ensure supplies
4. Never trip alone; first-timers need a sober trip-sitter
5. Avoid all polydrug use
6. Take at least the next day off for rest
7. Maintain multi-week intervals between trips
