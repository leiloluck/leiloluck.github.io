## Description

MDMA is an entactogen and amphetamine derivative (hydrochloride salt). Pure form: colorless/white crystals. "Ecstasy" refers primarily to tablets containing MDMA.

**Forms:** Tablets (2021 German median ~166 mg, ~10% exceeded 235 mg), crystals (typically >90% purity), powders/capsules (diluted with fillers).

**Common adulterants:** 2C-B, amphetamine, m-CPP, MDEA, MDA, caffeine, methamphetamine, mephedrone, methylone, eutylone, clephedrone, dipentylone.

## Routes & Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Oral | 30–60 min (max 90) | 4–6 hours |
| Intranasal | 5–15 min | 1–3.5 hours |

## Dosage (Upper Limits)

- Women: 1.3 mg/kg → ~80 mg for 60 kg
- Men: 1.5 mg/kg → ~110 mg for 75 kg

## Mechanism

Releases serotonin, noradrenaline, dopamine, and oxytocin.

## Effects

~20 min: Tingling, dry mouth, elevated heart rate, possible nausea/panic. ~60 min: Amplified emotions, closeness, euphoria, enhanced sensory perception.

## Risks

**Acute:** Cardiovascular activation, nausea, jaw clenching, thermoregulation disruption → heatstroke/collapse. Rare: rhabdomyolysis, kidney/liver damage, sudden cardiac death, seizures, cerebral infarction.

**Comedown:** Depressive mood, concentration problems, restlessness, anxiety (days).

**Long-term:** Possible serotonin neuron damage → memory impairment.

## Contraindications
Cardiovascular disease, psychiatric conditions, hyperthyroidism, epilepsy, diabetes, asthma, pregnancy/breastfeeding.

## Safer Use

1. Use drug checking; without it, max 1/3 tablet
2. Wait minimum 2 hours before redosing (PMA/PMMA risk)
3. Accept when effects fade — redosing has diminishing returns + increased side effects
4. Minimum 4 weeks between uses
5. 0.3–0.5L electrolyte drinks per hour
6. Take dancing breaks
7. Polydrug use much lower doses of each
8. Check medication interactions (SSRIs, MAOIs critical)

## Drug Combination Warnings
- PDE5 inhibitors ("Sextasy"): cardiovascular strain, priapism risk
- MDMA-induced vomiting may impair oral contraceptives and PrEP
