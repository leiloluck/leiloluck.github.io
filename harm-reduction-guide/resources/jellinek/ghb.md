## Description

CNS depressant ("downer"). Clear, slightly thick, very salty liquid. Typically in small ~5 cm plastic tubes (3–4.5 mL).

## Critical Dosing Danger

The margin between recreational and dangerous dose is **extremely narrow**. Loss of consciousness from ~3 grams, but purity varies widely (average ~377 mg/mL with wide variation). After one dose wears off subjectively, GHB remains active — **redosing at same amount increases total load → overdose risk**.

## Duration

Onset: 5–30 min. Duration: ~3 hours. Detection: blood ~8h, urine ~12h.

## Effects

**Psychological:** Euphoria, relaxation, disinhibition, talkativeness, sexual arousal, confusion, memory loss.
**Physical:** Heightened touch sensitivity, dizziness, headache, nausea, muscle weakness, involuntary sleep onset.

## Mechanism

Acts on GABA (inhibitory), glutamate (activating during comedown), dopamine (initial suppression then release), serotonin (euphoria).

## Risks

### Overdose — EXTREMELY DANGEROUS
- Respiratory disruption → respiratory arrest → death
- Choking on vomit while unconscious
- Users end up in ICU every year

### Alcohol Combination — EXTREMELY DANGEROUS
Even small GHB doses + alcohol → unconsciousness. Vomiting + aspiration compound danger.

### Dependence
- Can develop after just weeks of daily use
- Withdrawal: sweating, insomnia (days), trembling, anxiety, delirium with hallucinations
- **Withdrawal must occur under medical supervision**

### Other
- GBL (precursor) is stronger than GHB — dosing even harder
- Poorly made batches: pH can cause internal blistering

## Safer Use

1. **NEVER combine with alcohol** or other depressants
2. Know your dose — use measured tools; start very low with any new batch
3. Don't redose just because subjective effects faded (GHB still active)
4. Have sober companions who know overdose signs
5. Get batches tested where possible
6. Call emergency services immediately if someone loses consciousness
7. GBL is NOT interchangeable at same dose — more potent
