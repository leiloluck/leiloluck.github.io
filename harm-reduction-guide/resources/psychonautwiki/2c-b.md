# 2C-B — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/2C-B

## Description
4-Bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine (also known as Nexus, Bromo Mescaline, BDMPEA, Venus, and 2C-B) is a novel psychedelic substance of the phenethylamine class. It is the most well-known member of the 2C-x family, structurally related to mescaline. The mechanism of action involves serotonin receptor binding activity. Discovered in 1974 by Alexander Shulgin.

## Subjective Effects
- Open and closed-eye visuals
- Time distortion
- Euphoria
- Ego loss
- Effects described as moderate, warm, colorful, and highly sensual
- Less serious headspace than tryptamines (LSD, psilocybin)
- Greater emphasis on visual and tactile domain
- Under 15 mg: sensory and aesthetic enhancer (similar to MDMA)
- Above 15 mg: distinct psychedelic effect

## Dosage (Oral)
- Threshold: 2 mg
- Light: 5–15 mg
- Common: 15–25 mg
- Strong: 25–35 mg
- Heavy: 35+ mg

## Dosage (Insufflated)
- Threshold: 1 mg
- Light: 2–5 mg
- Common: 5–10 mg
- Strong: 10–15 mg
- Heavy: 15+ mg

## Duration (Oral)
- Total: 4–6 hours
- Onset: 20–60 minutes
- Come up: 15–30 minutes
- Peak: 1.5–2.5 hours
- Offset: 1–2 hours
- After effects: 2–4 hours

## Duration (Insufflated)
- Total: 2–4 hours
- Onset: 1–5 minutes
- Come up: 5–15 minutes
- Peak: 30–60 minutes
- Offset: 30–60 minutes
- After effects: 2–4 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Long-term health effects not studied in scientific context; exact toxic dose unknown.
- Anecdotal evidence suggests no negative health effects at low-to-moderate doses or with very sparing use.
- Generally thought to be physiologically well-tolerated; safety profile likely similar to classical psychedelics.
- Low abuse potential and toxicity.
- Adverse psychological reactions possible (anxiety, paranoia, delusions, psychosis), particularly for those predisposed to mental disorders.
- 2C-B is extremely dose-sensitive — small increases in dose can produce dramatic changes in intensity.

## Safer Use
- Start with very low doses due to extreme dose sensitivity.
- Use volumetric dosing for accuracy.
- Be aware that "Tusi" or "pink cocaine" marketed as 2C-B often contains completely different substances.
- Use reagent testing to verify substance identity.
- Use harm reduction practices.
