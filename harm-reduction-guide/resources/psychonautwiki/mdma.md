# MDMA — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/MDMA

## Description
3,4-Methylenedioxymethamphetamine (also known as ecstasy, E, XTC, emma, molly, mandy, pingers, and MDMA) is a classical entactogen substance of the amphetamine class. It is the most well-known and widely-used member of the entactogens, a diverse group that includes MDA, methylone, 4-MMC, and 6-APB. It produces its effects by promoting the release of serotonin, dopamine, and norepinephrine in the brain, particularly serotonin.

## Subjective Effects
- Stimulation
- Anxiety suppression
- Disinhibition
- Enhanced empathy and sociability
- Relaxation
- Euphoria
- Sexual dysfunction (including erectile dysfunction and delayed orgasm)

## Dosage (Oral)
- Threshold: ~30 mg
- Light: 40–75 mg
- Common: 75–140 mg
- Strong: 140–180 mg
- Heavy: 180+ mg

## Duration (Oral)
- Total: 3–5 hours
- Onset: 30–60 minutes
- Come up: 15–30 minutes
- Peak: 1.5–2.5 hours
- Offset: 1–1.5 hours
- After effects: 12–48 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Short-term risks include dehydration, bruxism, insomnia, hyperthermia, and hyponatremia.
- MDMA generally does not cause serious or life-threatening effects by itself unless associated with prolonged high ambient temperature, prolonged physical activity, poor water intake, or lack of acclimatization.
- Continuous activity without sufficient rest or rehydration may cause dangerous body temperature rise.
- MDMA may cause excessive thirst and inability to urinate, risking water intoxication and electrolyte imbalance.
- Neurotoxic at high doses; unclear how much this risk applies to typical recreational use.
- Tolerance builds unusually quickly; many users report it dramatically loses effectiveness with frequent use.
- Recommended to wait 1–3 months between each use to allow serotonin restoration and avoid toxicity.
- Using excessively high doses and multiple redosing is highly discouraged as it significantly increases toxicity.
- Moderate to high abuse potential but conversely low potential for genuine psychological dependence or addiction (lower than 10%).

## Safer Use
- Wait 1–3 months between uses.
- Avoid excessive redosing.
- Pay close attention to water intake — drink neither too much nor too little.
- Monitor body temperature and avoid overexertion.
- Avoid combining with alcohol (increases dehydration risk).
- Use harm reduction practices.
