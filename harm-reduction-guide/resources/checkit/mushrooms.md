## Description

"Magic Mushrooms" is a collective term for species containing psilocybin and psilocin (tryptamine-based hallucinogens). Found in at least 15 species across genera Psilocybe, Panaeolus, and Conocybe. Potency varies enormously within and between species.

## Effects

**Positive:** Tingling, warmth, laughter fits initially. Range from stimulating to strongly hallucinogenic. Perceptual changes across all senses. Dream-like reality. Deep connectedness with people and nature. Comparable to LSD but faster onset, shorter duration, generally more pleasant comedown.

**Negative:** Poisoning risk from misidentification. High doses: anxiety, terrifying visions, paranoid reactions. Physical: breathing difficulties, rapid heart rate, blood pressure changes, nausea, dizziness. Rare: fainting, seizures.

## Duration

- **Onset:** ~30 minutes
- **Peak:** ~2 hours
- **Total:** 4–6 hours
- Effect arrives in waves
- Dried mushrooms significantly more potent than fresh

## Dosage

Not possible to provide standard guidelines due to extreme variability between species, specimens, and preparation methods.

## Risks

- Tolerance buildup (cross-tolerance with LSD)
- Can trigger latent psychiatric conditions
- Misidentification with toxic species → life-threatening poisoning

## Contraindications
- Mental health issues, psychiatric history
- Cardiovascular problems, epilepsy
- Pregnancy

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ MDMA** | Greater temperature increase; may amplify MDMA neurotoxicity |
| **+ Alcohol** | Nausea, vomiting, circulatory problems, fainting |
| **+ Nicotine/Cannabis** | Circulatory problems, fainting |

## Safer Use

1. Only consume when psychologically/physically well, in safe environment
2. If anxious or uncertain — do not consume
3. Start low, wait 1–2 hours before considering redose
4. Never consume alone; first-timers need sober trip-sitter
5. Avoid dangerous situations (traffic, etc.)
6. Take next day off for rest; maintain weeks between uses
7. During bad trip: seek support; if not improving → professional help
