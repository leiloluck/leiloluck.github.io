## Description

MDMA (Methylen-Dioxy-Methyl-Amphetamin) belongs to the phenethylamine chemical family and is an amphetamine derivative. It appears in crystalline form, tablets (branded as "Ecstasy"), and less commonly as powder or in capsules.

Three primary pharmacological effects:
- **Empathogenic**: feelings of closeness and connection with others
- **Entactogenic**: amplifies one's own emotions and mood
- **Stimulating**: increases energy and alertness

Ecstasy tablets may contain substances beyond MDMA, including piperazines, research chemicals, various medications, or particularly dangerous compounds like PMA/PMMA.

## Effects

Onset (oral): ~20–60 minutes. Duration: ~4–6 hours.

**Initial physical effects:** Tingling throughout the body, dry mouth, dilated pupils, increased heart rate, possible tightness in the chest and nausea.

**Primary psychological effects:** Positive and negative emotions perceived more strongly; reduced inhibitions; emotional closeness and sense of belonging; euphoria; intensified body sensations (music perceived more intensely).

**Unpleasant effects:** Jaw clenching/cramps, muscle tremors, urinary retention, nausea, elevated blood pressure, heavy strain on heart/liver/kidneys, isolated epileptic seizures possible, significant anxiety and disorientation possible.

**Comedown:** Fatigue, exhaustion, confusion, sadness, and depressive mood.

## Mechanism of Action

MDMA triggers increased release of serotonin (primary), noradrenaline, and dopamine. The brain requires **up to four weeks** to rebuild normal serotonin levels.

## Dosage

| Group | Maximum Recommended | Example |
|-------|-------------------|---------|
| Men | 1.5 mg/kg body weight | 90 mg for 60 kg |
| Women | 1.3 mg/kg body weight | 78 mg for 60 kg |

Above these thresholds, dopaminergic system activation produces amphetamine-like effects. For nasal consumption, dosage should be significantly lower.

Without drug checking: no more than **one-third of a tablet** as initial dose, then wait at least **two hours** before re-dosing.

## Risks

### Acute
- **Overheating**: Prolonged dancing in warm environments → dangerous body temperature → collapse
- **Hyponatremia**: Excessive water intake without electrolytes, especially dangerous for women
- **Cardiovascular stress**: Elevated blood pressure, heart strain
- **PMA/PMMA danger**: Delayed onset → impatient redosing → life-threatening overdose

### Long-Term
- Intensive regular use may alter brain serotonin system (possibly permanently)
- Insecurity, anxiety, mood swings, sleep disturbances, exhaustion, depressive episodes
- Psychological dependence possible

## Contraindications — Never Use With:
- Heart problems or high blood pressure
- Anemia, thyroid disorders
- Liver or kidney damage, hepatitis
- Epilepsy, diabetes
- Pregnancy
- Psychological problems
- While taking antidepressants

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Alcohol** | Liver/kidney burden; dehydration; overheating risk; diminished MDMA qualities |
| **+ Antidepressants (MAOIs, tricyclics)** | ⚠️ Serotonin syndrome → seizures, loss of consciousness, collapse |
| **+ Speed** | Amplified neurotoxic effects; high fluid loss; depression lasting days |
| **+ Hallucinogens (LSD)** | Harmful to serotonergic system; depressive moods; cognitive impairment |
| **+ Caffeine** | Extreme cardiovascular strain; dizziness, racing heart |
| **+ Cannabis** | Can re-trigger MDMA effects; may cause intense anxiety/panic |

## Safer Use

1. Use drug checking services
2. Eat light food several hours beforehand
3. Without drug checking, start with max 1/3 tablet
4. Wait at least 2 hours before re-dosing
5. Stay within weight-based dose limits
6. Drink ~0.5L/hour of electrolyte drinks (NOT plain water only)
7. Avoid polydrug use
8. Take breaks from dancing
9. Maintain **4–6 weeks minimum** between uses
