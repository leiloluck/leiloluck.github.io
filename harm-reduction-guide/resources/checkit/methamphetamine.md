## Description

Methamphetamine (crystal, piko) is a powerful stimulant belonging to the phenylethylamine class, appearing as powder or crystals. It's typically consumed nasally or orally, occasionally smoked or injected.

## Effects

Produces "excessive urge to move and talk" with extreme wakefulness. Can cause euphoria, increased libido, and reduced hunger/thirst/pain perception. The substance triggers a "fight or flight" physiological response.

## Dosage & Duration

- **Typical dose:** 2.5-10mg
- **Onset:** nasal (15 min), oral (30-45 min), smoked/injected (seconds to 2 min)
- **Duration:** 6-30 hours depending on dose

## Acute Negative Effects

Irritability, aggression, anxiety, depression, jaw tension, dry mouth, fluid loss, urinary retention, and tremors. Risk of overestimating physical/mental capacity, leading to increased risk-taking.

## Long-term Risks

Psychological dependence with strong craving potential. Chronic use may cause weight loss, nutritional deficiencies affecting teeth/bones, circulation problems, menstrual cycle changes, nasal/stomach damage, skin inflammation, brain hemorrhage, heart attack, arrhythmias, and stroke. Possible "substance-induced psychosis" involving paranoia, delusions, and hallucinations.

## Contraindications

Avoid with: psychological problems/anxiety, hypertension, pregnancy/nursing, heart/circulatory conditions, liver/kidney damage, epilepsy, thyroid overactivity, elevated eye pressure.

## Safer Use Guidelines

- Use drug-checking services when available; test small amounts first
- Avoid redosing; oral consumption is safest method
- Don't share intranasal equipment (disease transmission risk)
- Drink non-alcoholic fluids (max ½ liter/hour)
- Take breaks, maintain cooling, eat light food with vitamins
- Plan consumption duration; avoid prolonging use to delay comedown
- Space out sessions with extended breaks
