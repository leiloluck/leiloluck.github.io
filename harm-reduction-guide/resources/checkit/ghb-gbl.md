## Description

GHB (4-Hydroxybutanoic acid) is related to neurotransmitter GABA and occurs naturally in the human body. Medical use: anesthetic and narcolepsy treatment. Despite street name "Liquid Ecstasy," has no chemical relationship to MDMA — pharmacologically more similar to alcohol. Typically sold as liquid.

**GBL** (gamma-butyrolactone) is immediately metabolized into GHB in the body, producing same effects.

## Effects (Dose-Dependent)

**⚠️ Concentration and purity vary dramatically — dose nearly impossible to estimate. Just a few drops can make an enormous difference.**

**Low doses:** Alcohol-like, anxiolytic, mildly euphoric, socially opening.
**Moderate:** Potentially sexually stimulating.
**Very high:** Strongly sedating. Sudden narcotic sleep — person can hardly be woken.

## Duration

1.5 to 3 hours.

## Risks

### Acute / Overdose
- **Respiratory depression → respiratory arrest → death**
- Enormously amplified by co-use with: benzodiazepines, opioids, pregabalin, **alcohol**

### Dependence
- Physical and psychological dependence with regular use
- Very frequent use (multiple daily doses) → severe dependence
- **Withdrawal** requires medical supervision: anxiety, panic, nausea, trembling, insomnia, delirium with severe hallucinations

## Contraindications
- Psychological problems, anxiety
- Pregnancy/breastfeeding
- High blood pressure, cardiovascular problems
- Liver/kidney damage, epilepsy

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| **+ Alcohol** | ⛔ EXTREMELY DANGEROUS — coma/death even at low GHB doses |
| **+ Benzodiazepines** | ⛔ EXTREMELY DANGEROUS — synergistic CNS depression |
| **+ Opioids** | ⛔ EXTREMELY DANGEROUS — respiratory arrest |
| **+ Pregabalin (Lyrica)** | ⛔ EXTREMELY DANGEROUS |
| **+ Any sedatives** | ⛔ EXTREMELY DANGEROUS |

## Safer Use

1. **NEVER combine with alcohol or any depressants** — risk of respiratory arrest
2. Avoid re-dosing — small increase can cause unwanted effects
3. Take regular breaks, drink non-alcoholic beverages (max 0.5L/hour)
4. Do not consume if feeling anxious
5. Practice safer sex
