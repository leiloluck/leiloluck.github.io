## Description

Potent stimulant from coca plant alkaloids. Street-level German purity (2021 median): 86.7% cocaine HCl. Common adulterants: phenacetin, caffeine, lidocaine, procaine, levamisole/tetramisole, paracetamol.

**Crack/Freebase:** Cocaine base created with baking soda or solvents. Uncharged molecules → rapid lung absorption → more addictive.

## Routes & Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration | Bioavailability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intranasal | 2–3 min | 30–60 min | 20–30% |
| Smoked (crack) | 8–10 sec | 5–10 min | 30–80% |
| Intravenous | 30–45 sec | 10–20 min | ~100% |
| Oral | 10–30 min | 2–3 hours | 20–40% |

## Dosage

- Intranasal: Medium 20–50 mg, High 50–100 mg
- Intravenous: 10–40 mg (as little as 25 mg may be fatal)

## Mechanism

Blocks reuptake of dopamine, norepinephrine, serotonin. Also blocks sodium channels → local anesthetic + cardiac arrhythmia risk.

## Effects

Euphoria, self-confidence, heightened concentration, suppression of hunger/fatigue, sexual stimulation.

## Risks

**Acute:** Elevated BP/HR, tremor, convulsions, nausea, cardiac arrhythmias, heart attack, cardiac arrest, stroke, kidney failure.

**Long-term:** Nasal damage/perforation, cocaine psychosis, heart muscle damage, dependence with intense craving.

## Dangerous Combinations
- **+ Alcohol**: Produces cocaethylene; masks intoxication → alcohol poisoning risk
- **+ Other stimulants**: Severe cardiovascular strain
- **+ Heroin (speedball)**: Very dangerous; increased mortality

## Safer Use

1. Get substances tested
2. Dose low, avoid frequent redosing
3. Snorting is lowest-risk route; own tube with rounded edges
4. For crack: use baking soda (NOT ammonia); use mouthpiece
5. IV: sterile equipment only; dose much lower than other routes
6. Change condoms every 30 minutes during extended sex
