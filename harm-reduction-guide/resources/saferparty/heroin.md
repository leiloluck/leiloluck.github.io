# Heroin (Diacetylmorphin/DAM) — SaferParty

**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** German (translated to English)

## Description

"A semi-synthetic substance belonging to the opioid group (painkillers) manufactured from raw opium."

## Effects

Pain relief, emotional regulation, sedation, anxiety reduction, and euphoria. Users experience an initial euphoric rush followed by well-being, indifference, calmness, and contentment.

## Duration by Route

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Injected/Smoked | Seconds | 2-5 hours |
| Snorted | Minutes | 2-5 hours |

Duration depends on dosage and substance purity.

## Dosage

Lethal dose for opioid-naive individuals: approximately 60mg pure heroin. Street heroin appears as white-to-brown powder; pharmaceutical forms (tablets/Diaphin) are approximately 5x more potent than street heroin due to pharmaceutical purity.

## Acute Risks

Breathing slowdown, nausea, vomiting, itching, low blood pressure, reduced heart rate, pinpoint pupils, urinary retention. "Overdose can reduce breathing frequency or cause respiratory arrest with potential brain and organ damage."

## Long-Term Risks

With regular use: confusion, memory gaps, slurred speech, coordination problems, severe constipation, decreased libido. High addiction potential with psychological and physical dependence. Withdrawal symptoms occur 8-12 hours after last use: sweating, chills, runny eyes/nose, vomiting, diarrhea, anxiety, depression, muscle cramps, insomnia.

### Route-Specific Risks
- **Snorting**: Nasal septum/mucous membrane damage; Hepatitis C exposure
- **Smoking**: Bronchi and lung damage; daily use causes lung adhesion
- **Injecting**: Vein inflammation; infection risks (Hepatitis C, HIV/AIDS, fungal infections)

## Safer Use Practices

- Start with low doses; wait before increasing
- Highest overdose risk after abstinence periods exceeding 2 days or unexpected purity increases
- Avoid injecting; use clean, sterile equipment exclusively; never share supplies
- Don't rely on dosing advice from regular users — their tolerance creates lethal doses for newcomers
- Maintain multi-day consumption breaks
- Avoid citrus fruits (especially grapefruit) during use — may enhance opioid effects or cause respiratory depression

## Drug Combinations (Dangerous)

- **Depressants** (alcohol, ketamine, GHB/GBL, nitrous oxide, benzodiazepines, other opioids): "High risk for vomiting and unconsciousness; suffocation danger is severe"
- **Methoxetamine**: Intensifies opioid effects
- **DXM**: Increased CNS disorder, cardiac, and respiratory problems; reduces opioid tolerance, raising overdose risk
- **Stimulants** (cocaine, amphetamine, methamphetamine): Extreme cardiovascular strain; effects may mask each other, creating delayed overdose risk as stimulant wears off
