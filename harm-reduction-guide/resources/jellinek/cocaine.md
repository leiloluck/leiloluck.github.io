## Description

Stimulant ("upper") from coca leaves. White crystalline powder. Routes: snorting (most common), injecting, smoking (crack only).

## Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Snorted | Minutes | 30–60 min |
| Smoked/Injected | 10–30 sec | 5–10 min |

## Effects

Increased energy/alertness, elevated mood/confidence, faster thinking, social desire, reduced appetite, nasal/oral numbing. Cravings stronger than with speed or MDMA.

## Risks

**Short-term:** Compulsive redosing, cardiovascular stress (blood clots, heart attack, stroke), nasal damage, excited delirium syndrome, hangover, panic attacks, psychosis.

**Long-term:** Personality changes, exhaustion, depression, aggression, lung damage (smoking), addiction (primarily psychological, rapid tolerance).

## Withdrawal Phases
1. Week 1: Intense mood swings
2. ~10 weeks: Period of overconfidence
3. Months 1–6: Depression, anhedonia, feeling like "outsider looking through glass wall"

## Safer Use

1. Test drugs — know strength and contaminants
2. Only use when feeling good
3. Use infrequently — only special occasions
4. Don't self-medicate the comedown
5. Eat and rest well before/after
6. Avoid polydrug use (alcohol + cocaine → cocaethylene → heart attack risk)
7. Don't drive
8. Use clean personal snorting tube; never banknotes
9. Avoid injecting/smoking routes
10. Heart/vascular risk increases with age
