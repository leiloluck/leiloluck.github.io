# Drug Alerts — The Loop (2015–2025)
**Source:** https://wearetheloop.org/drug-alerts
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** English

---

## Overview

The Loop provides drug checking at UK festivals and community settings. Their alerts cover substances "considered to present a significant threat," including missold drugs, contaminants, and unusually high-strength products.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Extremely High-Dose MDMA Pills Are Persistent (2016–2025)

Consistently found pills with 250–300+ mg MDMA (2–3× common dose):
- Punisher: 300+ mg
- Skype: 250–300 mg
- Donkey Kong: 250 mg
- Donald Trump: 250+ mg
- Ninja Turtle: 270 mg
- iPhone X: ~280 mg
- Duracell: ~250 mg
- Silver Skulls: 300 mg

### 2. Misselling and Substitution Are Rampant

Substances sold as MDMA frequently contain entirely different drugs:
- **Caffeine**: At Lost Village 2021, ~30% of "MDMA" samples were caffeine
- **4-CMC (Clephedrone)**: Found at multiple festivals (2021–2022)
- **N-ethylpentylone**: Repeatedly found as MDMA substitute (2018–2022) — longer negative effects (insomnia, paranoia)
- **Eutylone**: Brief MDMA-like effects then severe adverse effects
- **PMA**: Found in Superman pills (Boomtown 2019) — notoriously dangerous
- Identical pills contained different drugs by color: brown = MDMA, blue = 4-CMC, pink = eutylone

### 3. Nitazenes — Most Acute Current Danger (2025)

Pills confirmed to contain nitazenes associated with 2 deaths. Synthetic opioids far more potent than fentanyl. Represents extreme escalation in risk.

### 4. LSD Substitution

- **25x-NBOMe**: Sold as LSD (Boomtown 2018) — significant overdose risk
- **DOx compounds**: Effects lasting up to 48 hours

### 5. Other Alerts

- **2-FDCK**: Sold as ketamine (Durham 2018)
- **5F-MDMB-PICA**: Potent synthetic cannabinoid (Bristol 2018)
- **Pink tusi**: Poly-drug mixture marketed as psychedelic (Lost Village 2022)
- **Plaster of Paris**: Found as filler in pills (Secret Garden Party 2022)

---

## Consistent Harm Reduction Messages

- Seek medical help if unwell
- Drug checking services matter — identical pills can contain vastly different substances
- **Start low, go slow**
- Visual appearance is unreliable — same brand/logo = different substances across batches
