## Dosage by Route

**Intravenous (IV):**
- Common (no tolerance): 5-15mg
- Strong (no tolerance): 10-20mg
- Common (with tolerance): 20-30mg
- Strong (with tolerance): 30-50mg

**Smoked:**
- Common: 10-20mg
- Strong: 20-30mg

Note: "Purity varies wildly, and with such the dose does also."

## Duration by Route

**Intravenous:**
- Onset: 3-5 seconds
- Total duration: 4-5 hours

**Smoked:**
- Onset: 5-15 seconds
- Peak: 5-10 minutes
- Total duration: 3-5 hours

**General:** "When injected intravenously, smoked, or insufflated, heroin produces a wash of euphoria followed by a period of sedation lasting for 2-4 hours." Intramuscular and subcutaneous routes produce sedation lasting 3-5 hours without intense initial euphoria.

## Key Harm Reduction Guidelines

- Carry Narcan (overdose reversal medication)
- Avoid mixing with benzodiazepines or alcohol
- Never drive or operate machinery
- Critical warning: Users with reduced tolerance after abstinence face severe overdose risk at previous doses

## Notes on Potentiators

First-generation antihistamines may potentiate effects. Grapefruit juice does NOT potentiate heroin/morphine (contrary to common claims).
