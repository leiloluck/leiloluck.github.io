# Ketamine — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Ketamine

## Description
Ketamine (also known as Ket, K, Special K, vitamin K, Kitty) is a classical dissociative substance of the arylcyclohexylamine class. It is the archetypal dissociative, alongside PCP, methoxetamine, DXM, and nitrous oxide. The mechanism of action involves blocking of the NMDA glutamate receptor. Originally developed in 1963 as a replacement for the surgical anesthetic phencyclidine (PCP).

## Subjective Effects
- Motor control loss
- Pain relief
- Internal hallucinations
- Memory suppression
- Conceptual thinking
- Immersion enhancement
- Euphoria
- Depersonalization / dissociation
- At lower doses: alcohol-like disinhibition and relaxation
- At higher doses: hallucinogenic trance ("k-hole") — out-of-body or near-death experience

## Dosage (Insufflated)
- Threshold: 5 mg
- Light: 10–30 mg
- Common: 30–75 mg
- Strong: 75–150 mg
- Heavy: 150+ mg

## Duration (Insufflated)
- Total: 1–2 hours
- Onset: 2–5 minutes
- Come up: 5–15 minutes
- Peak: 15–45 minutes
- Offset: 30–60 minutes
- After effects: 4–12 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Moderate to high abuse potential; chronic use linked to compulsive redosing and psychological dependence.
- Both ketamine and esketamine may increase bladder damage.
- Reported cases of severe bladder and liver injury with ketamine use.
- Increasing evidence of bladder dysfunction (cystitis) from chronic use.
- Some evidence of cognitive and memory issues with chronic/heavy use.
- Insufflation can cause nasal damage, bleeding, and irreversible damage to nose and surrounding tissues with long-term repeated use.
- Sharing snorting equipment increases blood-borne disease risk.

## Safer Use
- Start with lower doses due to highly dose-dependent effects.
- Remain seated at higher doses to prevent injury from motor control loss.
- Prepare substances into fine powder before insufflating.
- Use your own clean snorting tool — never share.
- Rinse nose with saline within 30–60 minutes after use.
- Consider buccal administration as a harm reduction alternative.
- Avoid frequent/chronic use due to bladder damage risk.
- Use harm reduction practices.
