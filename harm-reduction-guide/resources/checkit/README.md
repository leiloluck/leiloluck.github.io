## About

checkit! is Vienna's competence center for recreational drugs ("Kompetenzzentrum für Freizeitdrogen"), operating since 1997. They provide:
- Anonymous, free, confidential drug checking (chemical analysis)
- Substance information and counseling
- Outreach at nightlife events and festivals
- Research and data collection

checkit! is one of Europe's longest-running drug checking services and a key reference for Central European harm reduction.

## Content Structure

Each substance page on checkit.wien typically contains:
- **Beschreibung** (Description) — chemical class, appearance, forms
- **Wirkung** (Effects) — desired and undesired effects, mechanism of action
- **Dauer** (Duration) — onset, peak, total duration by route
- **Dosierung** (Dosage) — dose ranges where available
- **Risiken** (Risks) — acute and long-term risks
- **Kontraindikationen** (Contraindications) — when never to use
- **Mischkonsum** (Drug Combinations) — specific interaction warnings
- **Risikoreduzierung** (Safer Use) — harm reduction guidelines
