# Cannabis — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Cannabis

## Description
Cannabis (also known as THC, marijuana, weed, pot, grass, hemp, kush) is a preparation of the cannabis plant that produces psychoactive effects when consumed (via smoking, vaporizing, or ingestion). It is the most used illicit substance in the world. The mechanism of action is binding activity at cannabinoid receptors distributed throughout the body. The principal psychoactive constituent is tetrahydrocannabinol (THC), one of 483 known compounds including at least 84 other cannabinoids (CBD, CBN, THCV, CBG).

## Subjective Effects
- Sedation
- Appetite enhancement
- Immersion enhancement
- Creativity enhancement
- Increased sense of humor
- Increased music appreciation
- Visual disconnection
- Euphoria
- At lower doses: relaxing depressant-like effects
- At higher doses: mild-to-moderate hallucinogenic effects (visual hallucinations, conceptual thinking, time distortion)
- Can either strongly suppress or enhance anxiety depending on individual and situation

## Dosage (Smoked)
- Threshold: 1 mg THC
- Light: 2–4 mg THC
- Common: 4–10 mg THC
- Strong: 10–25 mg THC
- Heavy: 25+ mg THC

## Dosage (Oral/Edibles)
- Threshold: 1 mg THC
- Light: 2.5–5 mg THC
- Common: 5–15 mg THC
- Strong: 15–30 mg THC
- Heavy: 30+ mg THC

## Duration (Smoked)
- Total: 1–4 hours
- Onset: 0–10 minutes
- Come up: 5–10 minutes
- Peak: 15–45 minutes
- Offset: 30–90 minutes
- After effects: 45–180 minutes

## Duration (Oral)
- Total: 4–10 hours
- Onset: 30–90 minutes
- Come up: 30–60 minutes
- Peak: 1–3 hours
- Offset: 1–3 hours
- After effects: 6–24 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Frequent use with >10% THC increases risk of developing psychotic disorder.
- Street cannabis averages 12% THC.
- PsychonautWiki recommends diluting cannabis buds with 3x hemp, hashish with 10x hemp.
- Acute adverse effects include anxiety, hyperemesis syndrome, impaired coordination/judgment, suicidal ideations, psychotic symptoms.
- Cannabinoid hyperemesis syndrome (CHS): recurrent nausea, vomiting, stomach cramp from prolonged high-dose use.
- Cannabis arteritis: very rare peripheral vascular disease.
- Use during brain development (pregnancy, adolescence) interferes with neurobiological systems.
- Meta-analysis shows lasting changes in attention/working memory networks after ~5 weeks abstinence.

## Safer Use
- Start with lower doses, especially edibles (onset can be delayed 30–90+ minutes).
- Weigh and dilute with hemp to control THC concentration.
- Avoid mixing with tobacco (nicotine is a stimulant).
- Use 5% THC products for pain relief with less intoxication risk.
- Use harm reduction practices.
