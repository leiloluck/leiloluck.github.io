## About

The Loop is the UK's first dedicated drug checking charity, delivering evidence-informed drug checking and harm reduction services. They operate:
- Drug checking at UK festivals and nightlife events (MULTI — Multi-Agency Safety Testing)
- Year-round onsite drug testing (since November 2024)
- London's first drug checking service (launched March 2026)
- Bristol's first regular drug checking service (launched January 2024)
- Training programs for event organizers and health professionals
- Research and publications

## Key Contribution: Drug Alerts

The Loop's primary value for this project is their **drug alerts database** — real-world testing results from UK festivals showing what substances actually contain. This provides crucial evidence for substance adulteration warnings.
