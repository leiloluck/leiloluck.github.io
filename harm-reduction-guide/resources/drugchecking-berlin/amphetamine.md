# Amphetamine / Speed — Drugchecking Berlin
**Source:** https://drugchecking.berlin/substanzen/amphetamin-speed
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** German (translated to English)

---

## Description

Stimulant, typically sulfate salt. Street purity varies enormously (0–100%); German 2021 seizure median ~18.7%. Caffeine is most common adulterant; 4-fluoroamphetamine and 4-methylamphetamine occasionally found.

**Forms:** Powder (most common), tablets, base/paste (claimed "fresh from lab" but usually just powder mixed with solvents).

## Duration

Effects last 4–12 hours.

| Route | Onset |
|---|---|
| Nasal | Within minutes |
| Oral | ~30 minutes |
| IV | Seconds |

## Dosage (Pure Amphetamine Sulfate)

| Level | Amount |
|---|---|
| Common | 10–25 mg |
| High (negative effects dominate) | 25–50 mg |

## Mechanism

Releases dopamine, noradrenaline, and (at high doses) serotonin. TAAR1 receptor involved in tolerance/dependence.

## Effects

Emergency/danger state activation: suppresses hunger/fatigue/pain, heightened wakefulness/concentration, increased performance/risk-taking, euphoria, boosted self-confidence. As effects wear off: increased sexual desire but delayed orgasm.

## Risks

**Acute:** Elevated temperature/BP/HR, jaw clenching, tremor, nausea, urinary retention, aggression, paranoia, seizures, circulatory collapse, cardiac arrest.

**Long-term:** Psychotic states, weight loss, skin inflammation ("speed pimples"), nasal damage, heart muscle damage, dependence.

**Detection:** Blood 8–34h, saliva 2–3 days, urine 1–4 days.

## Contraindications
Mental health problems, anorexia, MAOIs/tricyclics, cardiovascular problems, liver/kidney damage, epilepsy, diabetes, asthma, hyperthyroidism, glaucoma, pregnancy.

## Dangerous Combinations
- **+ Alcohol**: Masks sedation → alcohol poisoning risk
- **+ Other stimulants**: Severe cardiovascular/psychological strain
- **+ MAOIs/tricyclic antidepressants**: Life-threatening

## Safer Use

1. Test substances; without testing, start very small
2. Don't redose immediately
3. Plan session length; stop in time for recovery
4. Minimum 2-week break after weekend use
5. Stay hydrated with mineral/vitamin drinks
6. Oral is least harmful route (wrap in cigarette paper or capsule)
7. Own snorting tube with rounded edges if snorting
8. Take rest breaks to cool down
