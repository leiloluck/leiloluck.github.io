# Speed (Amphetamine) — Jellinek
**Source:** https://www.jellinek.nl/en/alcohol-drugs-behavior/speed-amphetamine/
**Date accessed:** 2026-03-31
**Language:** English

---

## Effects

Increased energy/alertness/wakefulness, euphoria, confidence, sociability, accelerated thinking, suppression of hunger/fatigue. Negative: restlessness, insomnia, jaw tension, nausea, irritability.

## Dosage

**Snorted:** Light 5–15 mg, Average 15–40 mg, Strong 40+ mg
**Swallowed:** Light 5–30 mg, Average 30–80 mg, Strong 80+ mg

## Duration

| Route | Onset | Effects | Residual insomnia |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snorted | 5–10 min | 3–6 hours | Up to 10–12h |
| Swallowed | 25–45 min | 6–8 hours | Up to 10–12h |

## Risks

**Short-term:** Overheating, dehydration, cardiovascular harm (internal bleeding, heart attack), hangover, weight loss, dental damage.

**Long-term:** Addiction/tolerance, depression, psychosis risk ("see, hear, believe things that aren't real").

## Safer Use

1. Test drugs for purity and adulterants
2. Only use when feeling good
3. Prevent overheating: ~1 glass water/soft drink/sports drink per hour; cooling breaks
4. Avoid combining with alcohol or other substances
5. Don't redose to counter comedown
6. Contraindicated: diabetes, heart problems, high BP, pregnancy, epilepsy, psychiatric conditions, MAOIs
7. Own clean snorting tube; never banknotes
8. Wet speed is dangerous — must be fully dried (residual solvents toxic)
