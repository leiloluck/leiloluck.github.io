## Description

White crystalline bitter-tasting powder derived from coca leaves. Frequently cut with adulterants on the black market. Can be snorted, injected, or converted to smokable forms (crack/freebase).

## Effects

**Desired:** Intense euphoria, heightened alertness, boosted self-confidence (can become recklessness), suppression of hunger/thirst/fatigue, elevated heart rate and blood pressure.

**High doses:** Anxiety, paranoid delusions, muscle cramps, tachycardia, arrhythmias. In extreme cases, cardiac arrest.

**Comedown:** Depressive mood, irritability, anxiety, strong craving to re-dose.

## Duration

| Route | Onset | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Intranasal (snorted) | A few minutes | 30–60 minutes |
| Smoked (crack/freebase) | Seconds | Up to ~10 minutes |
| Injected | Very rapid | Shorter than nasal |

## Mechanism

Blocks reuptake of dopamine into nerve cells, causing dopamine and noradrenaline accumulation in the synaptic cleft.

## Risks

### Dependence
- High psychological addiction potential
- Characteristic "binge" pattern: days of heavy use → exhausted abstinence → relapse
- Crack/freebase carry significantly higher dependence risk

### Psychological
- Anxiety disorders, personality changes, depression
- "Cocaine psychoses" with paranoid delusions and hallucinations

### Physical
- Chronic nasal mucosa inflammation; nasal septum perforation
- Coronary artery spasms and heart tissue damage even in young people → heart attacks
- Weight loss, chronic rhinitis
- Reduced libido; erectile dysfunction

### Common Adulterants
- **Levamisol**: Veterinary dewormer; causes agranulocytosis (white blood cell reduction → immune deficiency). Most dangerous cocaine adulterant. Converts to amphetamine-like aminorex in the body.
- **Local anesthetics (lidocaine, procaine)**: Increased cardiotoxicity.
- **Phenacetin**: Carcinogenic; kidney-damaging.

## Contraindications
- Cardiovascular disease, asthma, liver disease, hyperthyroidism
- Epilepsy
- Psychiatric conditions
- Pregnancy

## Drug Combinations

| Combination | Risk |
|---|---|
| **+ Alcohol** | Produces cocaethylene → severe cardiovascular strain; masks intoxication |
| **+ Other stimulants** | Extreme cardiovascular stress; life-threatening states |

## Safer Use

1. Use drug checking services
2. Start low, avoid rapid re-dosing
3. Take extended breaks to prevent dependence
4. Intranasal is safest route; injection most dangerous
5. Never share snorting equipment (hepatitis/HIV risk)
