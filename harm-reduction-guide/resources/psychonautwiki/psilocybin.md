# Psilocybin Mushrooms — PsychonautWiki

**Source:** https://psychonautwiki.org/wiki/Psilocybin_mushrooms

## Description
Psilocybin mushrooms (also known as magic mushrooms, psychedelic mushrooms, shrooms) are a family of psychoactive fungi that contain psilocybin, a psychedelic substance of the tryptamine class. The mechanism of action involves binding activity at serotonin receptors. Occur on all continents with over 200 taxonomically classified species; the most potent belong to the genus Psilocybe. Thought to have been used since before recorded history based on prehistoric rock art.

## Subjective Effects
- Visual geometry / hallucinatory states
- Time distortion
- Enhanced introspection
- Conceptual thinking
- Euphoria
- Ego loss
- "Earthy", "subliminal", or "dream-like" quality (vs. "stimulating" quality of LSD/mescaline/2C-B)
- More emotion enhancement, time distortion, and ego loss than LSD
- More nausea, confusion, and sedation than LSD

## Dosage (Oral — dried Psilocybe cubensis)
- Threshold: 0.25 g
- Light: 0.5–1 g
- Common: 1–2.5 g
- Strong: 2.5–5 g
- Heavy: 5+ g

## Duration (Oral)
- Total: 4–7 hours
- Onset: 20–60 minutes
- Come up: 15–30 minutes
- Peak: 1–2 hours
- Offset: 1–2 hours
- After effects: 4–24 hours

## Toxicity and Harm Potential
- Physiologically well-tolerated with extremely low toxicity relative to dose.
- No evidence for long-lasting effects on the brain or other organs.
- No documented deaths attributed to direct psilocybin toxicity.
- Can impair judgment and attention span, promoting erratic behaviors.
- Extreme negative reactions ("bad trips") can cause lasting psychological trauma if not managed.
- Evidence for mental health treatment only applies to controlled clinical settings; self-medicating may worsen issues.
- Low abuse potential; neither addictive nor physiologically toxic.
- Risk of consuming poisonous look-alike species when harvesting wild mushrooms.

## Safer Use
- Use under supervision of a tripsitter, especially for first time or higher doses.
- Start with a low-common dose; monitor for unusual reactions.
- Do not exceed the heavy dose range.
- Use reagent testing if using extracts or unknown products.
- Keep benzodiazepines or antipsychotics available to abort the trip if needed.
- CAUTION: If harvesting wild mushrooms, be absolutely certain of species identification — poisonous look-alikes can be fatal.
- Use harm reduction practices.
